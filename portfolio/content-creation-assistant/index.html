Content creation assistant

A concept design for a content creation digital assistant

Roles

  • Wireframing
  • Motion Design

In this project, I partnered with a visual designer to create a concept video for a digital assistant that guides users through content creation. My role was wireframing and motion design. I built and animated the prototype in Principle.

The assistant can take the spotlight as it walks the user through inputs step by step. At other times, it can integrate into a dynamic dashboard and provide contextual suggestions.
